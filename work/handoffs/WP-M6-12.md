## Summary of Changes

1. **Per-`(TenantId, principal_id)` Token-Bucket Rate Limiting & Cost Budgets (`crates/gap-sec/src/quota/bucket.rs`):**
   - `SubjectLimitStore` enforces deterministic token-bucket rate limiting (`50 rps`, burst `100` by default) and 60-second cost-unit budgeting (`600_000` cost units/min by default) with upfront `reserve_cost` and post-execution `reconcile_cost` refunds/overages (`RUN-050`), returning `ErrorCode::RateLimited` (`GAP_RATE_LIMITED`) with populated `retry.after_ms`.
   - `evict_oldest_if_full` deterministically bounds tracked subject maps at `65_536` entries to prevent unbounded memory growth under subject-explosion attacks (`TM-11`).

2. **RAII Branch-Share & Ingestion Concurrency Quotas (`crates/gap-sec/src/quota/concurrency.rs`):**
   - `ConcurrencyQuotaStore` issues RAII `BranchConcurrencyPermit` enforcing per-tenant share ceilings per `(TenantId, BranchId)` (`5_000` basis points = `50%` of `branch_capacity` by default) and RAII `IngestConcurrencyPermit` (`max_ingest_per_tenant = 4` by default), decrementing active counts deterministically on drop.

3. **Tenant Storage & S1 Inference Budgets + Unified Enforcer (`crates/gap-sec/src/quota/{budgets,mod}.rs`):**
   - `TenantResourceBudgetStore` enforces per-tenant storage ceilings (`5_000_000` rows, `1 GiB` blobs) and sliding 60-second S1 inference call limits (`600` calls/min) while `QuotaMetrics` records lock-free atomic counters (`QuotaMetricsSnapshot`).
   - `QuotaEnforcer` composes all quota dimensions behind `Arc<dyn Clock>` (`SystemClock` in production, `ManualClock` in tests) with strict `TenantId` isolation (`INV-09`, `SEC-050`).
