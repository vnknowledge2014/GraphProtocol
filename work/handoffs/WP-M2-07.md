## Observations & Cross-Crate Notes (`AGENT-GUIDE §9`)
1. `adapters/gap-mcp/src/inbound/discovery.rs::execute_gap_search` keys its `visible_catalog` lookup solely by `Query.<op>` (`ElementDoc.path`), whereas `AssembledGroups::build_group_element_docs` (`crates/gap-snapshot/src/groups/mod.rs`) populates `ElementDoc.element_id` with `GroupId` (`grp_...`) and puts the operation ID in `ElementDoc.operation_id`. Inside `bench/gapbench/src/synth.rs`, the harness preserves `assembled_groups.apply_to_snapshot(...)` groups/operations while keying its `HybridElementIndex` documents by `doc.path` so both `McpFacade` and `RouteDiscoveryService::search` resolve every hit cleanly without editing outside `WP-M2-07` `owns`. Follow-up in a `gap-mcp`-owning WP: resolve `visible_catalog` via `hit.operation_id` as well as `hit.element_id`.
2. During compiled release testing right after workspace builds, CPU contention pushed `N=1000` `__search` p95 from steady-state `12.304 ms` up to `65.282 ms`; inspecting `crates/gap-route/src/lib.rs` showed `search()` runs `project_visible_schema(...)` over the full `apollo_compiler::Schema` on every `__search` call. Steady-state standalone release execution (`./target/release/gapbench`) passes with `p95 = 12.304 ms` (`<= 40.0 ms`), and caching per-principal schema projections in `gap-route` is a worthwhile optimization note for M3+/R1.
