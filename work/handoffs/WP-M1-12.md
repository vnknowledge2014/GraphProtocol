Design notes & brief recordings (`work/context/m1-12-brief.md`):
  1. [Deterministic assembly & `BranchFactory` seam (`Brief §3.1–§3.2`, `ARC-002`, `ARC-007`, `INV-01`, `INV-15`)]:
     `ServerFile::load` delegates to `GapConfig::load_from_path_with_process_env`, checks UID uniqueness,
     and pre-reads every relative OpenAPI spec (`branches[i].openapi.spec`) before connector startup.
     When `uid` is omitted, `derive_branch_uid` hashes `"gap.branch-uid.v1:" || branch.id` with `blake3`
     into an RFC 9562 UUIDv7 (`BranchUid::from_unix_ms`). `DefaultBranchFactory` wraps `OpenApiConnector`
     and `McpConnector` in `ServerCore` + `in_memory_grpc` (`OPS-003` (c)) and calls startup `Describe`;
     any branch or composition error aborts startup naming the branch and error code without panicking or
     echoing secret values. `McpFacade` uses `HideT2` in `prod` and `AllVisible` in `dev`, shares
     `TransportEngine` + `SnapshotHandle` + `draining`, and is mounted via `AppState::with_http_module`.
  2. [Two-stage `Shutdown` & `serve --stdio` (`Brief §3.3`, `RUN-090`, `SEC-004`, `OPS-011`)]:
     `Shutdown` forwards `SIGTERM`/`SIGINT` over a bounded `mpsc::channel(8)`: the first signal stores
     `draining = true` (`SeqCst`, flipping `/readyz` to `503`) and resolves `ShutdownTrigger` for
     `gap_transport::serve` (`shutdown_grace = [server].shutdown_grace_ms`); a second signal during drain
     invokes `on_force_exit(130)`. `serve_stdio_with_streams` attaches a scoped `tracing::Dispatch` to
     `log_writer` (`stderr` in production) and authenticates each JSON-RPC line with a fresh pinned
     `Snapshot` and a `PrincipalKind::Service` principal carrying the configured tenant scopes.
  3. [`gap` CLI & `examples/walking-skeleton` (`Brief §3.4–§3.5`, `OPS-044`, `SEC-062`, `SEC-070`, `SEC-072`)]:
     `gap init` embeds the five `examples/walking-skeleton/` files at compile time (`include_str!`),
     validates canonical confinement under `DIR`, refuses symlinks via `symlink_metadata`, and writes
     exclusively with `OpenOptions::create_new(true)` (`O_CREAT | O_EXCL`). `gap doctor` checks config
     parsing, referenced OpenAPI/secret files, `server.listen` parseability + non-wildcard posture in
     `prod`, non-empty `api_keys` in `prod`, `T2` branch warnings, and `stdio` MCP binary existence +
     `sha256` digest via `gap_mcp::outbound::launch::verify_binary`, marking `runtime.toolchain`,
     `security.sandbox`, and `models.s1` as `skipped (not available in M1)`.
