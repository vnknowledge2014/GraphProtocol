## Summary

- Implemented Gate `G4` data-driven join verification, bottom-k union sketch estimation, and the `PROPOSED -> VERIFIED -> CANARY -> ACTIVE` join lifecycle in `crates/gap-ingest/src/gates/join_verify/mod.rs`:
  - **`NGC-050`, `NGC-051`, `NGC-052`:** `JoinSketchContext`, `build_sketch_request` (validating non-empty tenant ID, non-empty tenant salt, `PrincipalKind::Service`, and strictly read-only scopes; clamping `sample_size` to `100_000`), `normalize_sketch_value`, and `estimate_bottom_k_containment` (`|bottomk(A) ∩ bottomk(B)|` over the smallest `k_eff = min(k, |A|, |B|)` elements of the union normalized by LHS union coverage, plus exact mode when `exact == true`, and standard error bounds).
  - **`ADR-012`, `ING-040`, `ING-021`, `INV-06`:** `evaluate_join_g4` enforcing `containment >= 0.95`, `null_ratio <= 0.50`, RHS uniqueness `>= 0.999` (or declared PK/unique key), `sample_count >= 1_000` (or non-empty exact full domain), compatible value kinds, and cardinality consistency. Empty or missing-sample domains stay `PROPOSED` without promoting. `JoinLifecycleRecord` enforces `INV-06` (`visible_to_planner == true` iff `Active`), `ING-021` (LLM candidates require explicit admin approval), and `ING-041` canary promotion (`>= 100` executions or `>= 10 min`) and auto-revert + alert on `> 10` percentage point drop.
- Implemented Gate `G8` regression replay in `crates/gap-ingest/src/gates/replay/mod.rs`:
  - `evaluate_gate_g8_replay` replays up to top-N (default `200`) cached/persisted operations and conformance fixtures against a candidate `CISG` plus optional read-only `ShadowReplayObservation`s (verifying `read_only == true` and `response_discarded == true`), flagging breaking schema/plan diffs unless explicitly overridden by `breaking_change_ack`.
- Implemented execution-plane shadow canary evaluation in `crates/gap-exec/src/shadow/mod.rs`:
  - `ShadowCanaryRunner` limits shadow sampling to read-only `OpKind::Query` traffic, records and discards shadow RHS payloads so client responses are untouched, and emits `ContinueCanary`, `PromoteToActive` (`>= 100` executions or `>= 10 min`), or `RevertToVerified { alert }` (`> 10` pp drop or elevated failure rate).
- Added 5 adversarial & valid join fixtures in `tests/fixtures/joins/`:
